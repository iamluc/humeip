Director

Francois Vieillescazes

Francois Vieillescazes is an Intellectual Property attorney with 20 years’ experience in the field of trademarks, designs, patents, and IP transactions in Singapore and Asia. He has been working as an independent IP Consultant in Singapore since 2018, building upon years of prior experience with a local law firm.

Previously, Francois began his professional journey as a litigation lawyer in Paris, before joining the Trademarks department of a major law firm in South Korea, and later heading the Shanghai office of a prominent French IP firm.
