Director
Photo

Francois Vieillescazes

Francois Vieillescazes is an Intellectual Property attorney with 20 years’ experience in the field of trademarks, designs, patents, and IP transactions in Singapore and Asia.

After starting his career as a litigation lawyer in Paris, Francois joined the IP Department of a large law firm in Korea, worked for law firms in Singapore, and headed the Shanghai office of a French IP firm. He has worked as an independent IP Consultant in Singapore since 2018.
